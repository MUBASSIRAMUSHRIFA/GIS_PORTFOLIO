# GIS Portfolio – Wayanad Study

This portfolio showcases Geological Mapping of Kattikkulam Gabbro and Associated Rocks_Wayanad,Kerala,India
## Maps
- **Map 1: Geological map of study area**  
  Covers the area view of rock types of study area and points marks the sampled location, which is focused on gabbro.
- **Map 2: Location map of study area**  
  This map shows the exact location of study area in india and show how roads and water bodies are connected

## Tools Used
ArcMap, Spatial Analysis, Cartography, GPS
